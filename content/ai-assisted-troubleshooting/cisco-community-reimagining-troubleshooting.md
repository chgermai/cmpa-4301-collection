**What it covers:** This is a discussion thread started by a Cisco engineer. In it, the engineer argues that troubleshooting is harder to automate than tasks like software upgrades, because it depends on judgment and decision-making rather than a fixed process from a run book. The author suggested the idea of running multiple AI agents on the same problem at the same time.

**Why it's valuable:** It's a useful alternative opinion compared to the academic and vendor sources in the collection. It shows how practicing network engineers are thinking through the problem in real time, including the skepticism and open questions, not just the finished pitch.

**Who would find it useful:** Engineers and managers who want a sense of where the industry conversation is right now, including the doubts, rather than a vendor's confident framing.

**Limitations:** It's an open-ended discussion, not a resource with conclusions. There's no data or working example here, it is just questions and anecdotes, and the thread could go stale or stop getting replies.
