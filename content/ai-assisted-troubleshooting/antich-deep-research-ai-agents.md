**Source:** Antich, J. (2025). *Revolutionizing Network Troubleshooting with Deep Research AI Agents.* Cisco Blogs. https://blogs.cisco.com/sp/revolutionizing-network-troubleshooting-with-deep-research-ai-agents

**What it covers:** The first of three posts by a Cisco product management engineer, proposing "Deep Network Troubleshooting," which is a system of specialized AI agents that plan an investigation, search across CLI, API, and telemetry sources, and verify findings before issuing a diagnosis.

**Why it's valuable:** It's a more engineering-level look at agentic troubleshooting than other pages in this collection. The multistep "plan, search, verify, refine" framing and the specific agent roles provides a clear picture of what an agentic troubleshooting system must do.

**Who would find it useful:** Engineers designing or evaluating their own agentic troubleshooting workflow that want to see how the problem gets broken down into simple, verifiable steps.

**Limitations:** This is still written by a Cisco engineer on Cisco's blog, so it's describing Cisco's own approach rather than an independent comparison. It is important to keep this framing in mind.
