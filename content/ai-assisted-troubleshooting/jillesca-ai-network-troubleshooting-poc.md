**Source:** Jillesca. (2024). *AI-Network-Troubleshooting-PoC* [GitHub repository]. https://github.com/jillesca/AI-Network-Troubleshooting-PoC

**What it covers:** A working proof-of-concept showing how an LLM responds to a Grafana alert by connecting to a network device, diagnosing the root cause, and suggesting a remediation step.

**Why it's valuable:** This was presented at Cisco DevNet events in 2024 and is a real, runnable project rather than a marketing document. It's a concrete example of how an agentic workflow for network troubleshooting can actually be built, end to end.

**Who would find it useful:** Engineers who want a concrete starting point for how AI can benefit their team, or who need a working proof-of-concept to support a case for executive buy-in.

**Limitations:** The repository is archived, and the pace of change in this space means the specific implementation could go stale quickly. It's more useful as a reference architecture than as production-ready code.
