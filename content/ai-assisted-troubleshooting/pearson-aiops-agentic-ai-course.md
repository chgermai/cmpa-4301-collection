**Source:** Halley, J. (2026). *AI Operations (AIOps): Using Agentic AI to Operate Your Network* [Video course]. Pearson IT Certification. https://www.pearsonitcertification.com/store/ai-operations-aiops-using-agentic-ai-to-operate-your-9780135493335

**What it covers:** This is a six-lesson video course covering agentic AI fundamentals, Day 1/Day 2 network operations, agent architectures, and distributed agent deployment. It's built for NOC/SOC teams and assumes existing networking or IT operations experience.

**Why it's valuable:** It's a structured, but also paid, professionally produced alternative to piecing this topic together from blog posts and papers. The class goes lesson by lesson through agent architecture and deployment. It has a level of depth the other sources in this collection don't provide.

**Who would find it useful:** Network operations staff who want a guided, structured introduction into agentic AI concepts rather than scattered reading, and who are willing to pay for the course.

**Limitations:** It's a paid product ($239.99 at list), so it has a financial commitment rather than the free sources here. It's also new (published May 2026), so there isn't yet much independent feedback on how well it holds up in practice.
