**Source:** DCT Technology. (2025). *AI in Network Management: Can Your Network Fix Itself?* DEV Community. https://dev.to/dct_technology/ai-in-network-management-can-your-network-fix-itself-2f48

**What it covers:** The author of this article argues that AI is shifting network management from reactive troubleshooting to proactive, self-healing systems. It includes a Python example for anomaly detection and a YAML automation snippet for responding to a latency incident, plus a scenario describing AI detecting a DDoS attack and automatically blocking traffic and scaling bandwidth.

**Why it's valuable:** It's a more understandable, code-first entry than the academic papers in this collection. Seeing a concrete (and simplified) anomaly-detection script and remediation workflow side by side helps make the abstract idea of "self-healing networks" concrete for someone who hasn't built one.

**Who would find it useful:** Engineers who want a beginner-friendly, example-driven introduction before diving into academic and vendor material.

**Limitations:** Published by an IT consulting firm, so it reads partly as a capability showcase. The code examples are illustrative, not production patterns, and the DDoS scenario is described rather than demonstrated.
