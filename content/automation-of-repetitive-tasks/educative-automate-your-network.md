**Source:** Educative. (n.d.). *Automate Your Network* [Interactive course]. https://www.educative.io/courses/automate-your-network/m73KD14ZZ1A

**What it covers:** This is a 13-section interactive course on replacing manual network administration tasks with automation. The course covers Ansible, Git, Linux, and CI/CD pipelines, with hands-on exercises throughout.

**Why it's valuable:** It's structured around the everyday repetitive work network engineers do — config management, documentation, troubleshooting — rather than presenting automation as a concept. The hands-on format gives an engineer the ability to write and run automation, instead of just reading about it.

**Who would find it useful:** Engineers moving from manual network administration toward code-driven and automated workflows. Especially those who want a structured, hands-on path rather than piecing tutorials together themselves.

**Limitations:** It's a general network automation course, not AI-specific. Since AI/LLM-driven automation isn't the focus, this is more of a foundation-building resource.
