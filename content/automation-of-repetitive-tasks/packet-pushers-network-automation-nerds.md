**Source:** Chou, E. (Host). *Network Automation Nerds* [Podcast]. Packet Pushers. https://packetpushers.net/podcast/network-automation-nerds/

**What it covers:** A bi-weekly podcast covering a wide range of topics in network automation, including how teams are adopting AI into their automation workflows.

**Why it's valuable:** Episodes are built around interviews with practitioners who talk through real deployments and the tradeoffs behind their decisions, rather than a pitch for a single product. The back catalog is a valuable resource for finding tools and case studies that come up nowhere else in this collection.

**Who would find it useful:** Engineers who want an ongoing, conversational way to stay exposed to how AI tools are actually being adopted in the field, rather than a one-time read.

**Limitations:** Quality varies episode to episode, and the guest list is a mix of vendors and independent practitioners, so some episodes lean more promotional than others.
