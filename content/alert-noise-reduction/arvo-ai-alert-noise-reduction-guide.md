**Source:** Casarotto-Dinning, N. (2026). *Automated Alert Noise Reduction: 2026 Guide.* Arvo AI. https://www.aurorasre.ai/blog/automated-alert-noise-reduction

**What it covers:** A guide breaking alert noise reduction into three approaches — rule-based suppression, ML-based correlation/deduplication, and AI-driven investigation and triage. It includes specific recommendations like reviewing suppression rules regularly and measuring success by pages-per-shift rather than raw alert counts.

**Why it's valuable:** It provides a clear breakdown of the different techniques for reducing alert noise, rather than just claiming a product does it. The recommendation to measure human outcomes instead of alert-count reduction is a useful, concrete framing for evaluating any tool in this category.

**Who would find it useful:** Anyone trying to build a mental model of the different approaches to alert noise reduction before evaluating specific products, including the open-source and vendor options elsewhere in this collection.

**Limitations:** Written by the CEO of Arvo AI to promote their own open-source tool, Aurora. The three-approach framework is useful, but the guide's comparisons to competitors should be read with that in mind.
