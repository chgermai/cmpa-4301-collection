**Source:** Gam, A. (n.d.). *awesome-ai-sre* [GitHub repository]. https://github.com/agamm/awesome-ai-sre

**What it covers:** A curated, community-maintained list of 100+ AI-powered tools and resources for site reliability engineering, organized into 14 categories including incident management, AIOps platforms, log analysis and anomaly detection, and runbook automation.

**Why it's valuable:** It functions as a map of the whole landscape rather than a single tool or opinion, which makes it a good jumping-off point for finding more sources beyond what's already in this collection, including tools specifically built for alert correlation and anomaly detection.

**Who would find it useful:** Anyone doing their own research into this space who wants a broad, categorized starting point rather than relying on whatever a search engine surfaces first.

**Limitations:** It's a list, not an evaluation, so inclusion doesn't mean a tool is good, well-maintained, or a fit for network engineering specifically (a lot of the entries are general SRE/DevOps tools, not network-specific). It also needs regular maintenance to stay current, and how actively it's updated over time is worth checking before relying on it long-term.
