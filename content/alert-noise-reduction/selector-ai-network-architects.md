**Source:** Selector AI. (n.d.). *Network Architects.* https://www.selector.ai/network-architects/

**What it covers:** Selector AI's Network Architects page pitches a real-time platform that turns network diagrams and telemetry data into a what-if simulation environment, running forecasting and anomaly detection before an outage actually happens.

**Why it's valuable:** For alert noise specifically, Selector claims the ability to correlate data across more than 300 integrations to reduce mean time to resolution and eliminate noisy, duplicate alerts. That lets a team stay focused on the actual problem instead of chasing down alert storms when it matters most.

**Who would find it useful:** Network architects evaluating this category of product who want to understand what a real-time correlation and simulation platform is supposed to do before bringing it in for a demo or trial.

**Limitations:** This is a vendor marketing document. There are no independent benchmarks or third-party validation of the correlation or MTTx claims, so the numbers should be treated as a starting point for questions, not as proof.
